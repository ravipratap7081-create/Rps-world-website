# Diwali Business Promo Pack — Pricing

## Proposed price

**Launch price: ₹149** (introductory)
**Regular price: ₹199** (after launch period)

Single price point. No fake strikethrough discounts, no inflated "MRP".

## Rationale

1. **Priced against the actual contents, not against custom work.**
   The pack is 12 original creatives (6 square + 6 vertical, print-ready
   1080px) plus 30+ ready-to-post captions in Hindi/Hinglish/English —
   enough for a small shop's entire Diwali campaign (announcements,
   greetings, reminders, urgency, WhatsApp broadcasts, Instagram).
   The custom-design service kit sits at ₹299/₹499/₹999 because a human
   designs bespoke work per client. This pack is self-service and
   instant-delivery with zero manual fulfilment, so it must sit clearly
   below ₹299 — ₹149 does that with daylight to spare.

2. **₹149 converts for the target buyer.** A small shop owner in Kanpur
   will not deliberate over ₹149 the way they would over ₹299 — it's an
   impulse buy, roughly the price of a plate of chaat. At ₹199 regular,
   the launch price still feels like a genuine early-bird reward rather
   than a gimmick.

3. **Honest launch/regular split, no fake discount theatre.** ₹149 →
   ₹199 is a real, small introductory gap (25% off), stated plainly.
   No "₹9999 crossed out" nonsense — the buyer's trust matters more than
   a one-time conversion trick, especially for a brand selling to small
   businesses on reputation.

4. **Unit economics work without manual effort.** At ₹149, even modest
   volume pays: 100 buyers = ₹14,900 for a product that costs nothing
   per additional copy. No designer hours, no revisions, no WhatsApp
   back-and-forth per order — pure margin after the one-time creation cost.

## What the price is NOT

- Not a subscription, not a per-design fee — one payment, full pack.
- Not negotiable per buyer (keeps fulfilment zero-touch).
- Price changes, if any, will be announced — never via fake countdowns
  or fabricated discounts.
