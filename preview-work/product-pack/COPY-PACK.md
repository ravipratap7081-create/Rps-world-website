# Diwali Business Promo Pack — Copy Pack

30+ ready-to-use promotional texts for small businesses' Diwali marketing.
All texts are original and brand-neutral — post as-is on WhatsApp, Instagram,
Facebook, or print. Add your shop name / phone number where a natural fit exists
at the end of a message; none of the texts require it to make sense.
