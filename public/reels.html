Monthly packages — proposed scope

Proposal — not final pricing

The tiers below are a proposal built from live agency benchmarks. They are not published prices yet — the scope is being finalised before anything is charged at these rates.

₹999 — Two finished reels

₹499.50 per reel — the same rate as the pilot
  • Two finished reels, up to 30 seconds each, from your footage
  • Hook + captions + music on both
  • One revision round per reel
  • Delivered within 5 days

Why two reels, not more? The old idea of more reels for ₹999 made each reel cheaper than the ₹499 single-reel pilot — that prices the work backwards and teaches buyers a reel is worth very little. At ₹999 for two reels the per-reel rate stays exactly the pilot rate, so the step up is “try a second reel”, not a discount. Two reels also show whether the style works across two different offers before a monthly commitment.

Retainer — ₹7,999 / month

8 reels a month · ₹1,000 per reel
  • 8 finished reels a month, up to 30 seconds each
  • Hook + captions + hashtags included on every reel
  • Two revision rounds per reel
  • 48-hour turnaround per reel, from approved brief + footage
  • Monthly content plan — 4 reel ideas matched to your offers
  • Posting on your Instagram after your approval, on request
Why these numbers?

Checked live on 17 Nov 2026: Reelkraft’s Starter plan is ₹15,000/month for 8 reels (2 revision rounds, 48-hour turnaround) — about ₹1,875 per reel. ReelScale’s Plus plan is ₹14,499/month for 6 reels (1 revision per reel) — about ₹2,417 per reel. Those are agency rates, not RPS rates — they are shown here only as the benchmark. RPS is a one-person Kanpur studio with lower overhead, so the proposed retainer works out at ₹1,000–₹1,250 per reel: roughly half the agency benchmark, while staying well above the one-time ₹499 introductory pilot. The pilot is deliberately below the ongoing rate — it is a first-try price, and it only ever applies once.

Agreed before we start

Footage: you supply it — phone-shot clips sent on WhatsApp or Drive. No shoot is included in any tier above.

Revision: one round means text, music or timing changes on the same edit. A new concept, new footage or a re-shoot is a new reel, not a revision.

Approval: you — the business owner — approve each reel before it is delivered or posted.

Posting: RPS does not post to your account unless you ask. On the ₹7,999 retainer, posting after your approval is included on request.
