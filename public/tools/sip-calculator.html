SIP me compounding kaise kaam karta hai?
Har mahine ka investment market me grow hota hai, aur us growth par bhi growth milti hai. Lamba time = zyada compounding. Isiliye 10 saal ki SIP 5 saal ki do SIPs se zyada deti hai.

12% return realistic hai?
