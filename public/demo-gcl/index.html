GCL Method

practice zyada.

Zyadaatar jagah notebook bhar jaati hai, zubaan nahi khulti. GCL me ulta hota hai — class ka zyadaatar time tum bolte ho, sirf sunte nahi.

Aam coaching vs GCL

Paisa lagane se pehle ye fark samajh lo — kyunki English bolna ek skill hai, subject nahi.

Aam jagah kya hota hai

  • 50–60 students ki bheed — bolne ka number hi nahi aata
  • Grammar ke rules rataye jaate hain, bolna nahi sikhaya jata
  • Galti par hasi udti hai — student aur chup ho jata hai
  • Fees pehle, result ka koi hisaab nahi

GCL me kya milta hai

  • Chhota batch — har student roz bolta hai, bina number lagaye
  • Bolne par focus — grammar bolte-bolte khud sahi hoti hai
  • Galti = progress. Yaha galti karne wale ko encourage karte hain
  • Pehle free demo class — pasand aaye tabhi admission
