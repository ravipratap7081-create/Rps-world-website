Aam coaching vs American School of Language

Paisa lagane se pehle ye fark samajh lo — kyunki English bolna ek skill hai, subject nahi.

Aam jagah kya hota hai

  • 50–60 students ki bheed — bolne ka number hi nahi aata
  • Grammar ke rules rataye jaate hain, bolna nahi sikhaya jata
  • Galti sudharne par utna dhyaan nahi milta
  • Fees pehle, progress ka hisaab saaf nahi milta

Yahan kya milega (sample)

  • Roz bolne ki practice — class me bolna, sirf sunna nahi
  • Bolne par focus — grammar bolte-bolte khud sahi hoti hai
  • Galti = progress. Yaha galti karne wale ko encourage karte hain
  • Pehle details poochho — pasand aaye tabhi admission
